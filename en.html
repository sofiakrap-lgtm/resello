FAQ

Do all the old items need to be photographed again?

No. Old items are sold with their current tags, and new bookings go into Resella.

Does the till work if the internet goes down?

Yes. Sales are saved and synced when the connection is back.

What devices do I need?

A tablet, a scanner, a receipt printer and a cash drawer. We bring and install them, so you do not need to buy anything yourself.

Can I use my own logo and colours?

Yes. The sellers' booking page shows your shop's look.

How long does onboarding take?

Usually 1-3 days. We do the installation with you.

What does Resella cost?

The price depends on the size of your shop. See an estimate in the price calculator, and we go through the exact price in the demo.

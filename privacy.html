2. Who this applies to

  • Visitors to our website
  • People who book a demo, join the waitlist or contact us
  • Our customers, meaning second-hand shops and their contact persons
  • Resella users, such as shop staff, sellers and buyers

3. Data of a shop's sellers and buyers

When a second-hand shop uses Resella's till and management system, the shop is the controller of its staff's, sellers' and buyers' data, such as user accounts, spot bookings, sales, payouts and bank details. Resella processes this data on behalf of the shop and according to its instructions. This is agreed with the shop in a data processing agreement.

If you are a seller or a buyer, contact your own shop first about your data. We help the shop answer your request.

Resella itself is the controller only for the data of this website, our marketing, customer contracts and invoicing. This policy covers that data.

4. What data we collect

  • Contact details: name, email, phone number and the name of the shop
  • Demo booking: the time you chose and your message
  • Waitlist: email, postal code, whether you are a finder, seller or shop owner, what you look for most and the local flea market you recommend (both optional) and how you found the site (for example Instagram or a QR code)
  • Newsletter and Kirppiskatsaus report: email and the region whose report you subscribe to
  • Customer relationship: the shop's contact person, the contract and invoicing details
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device. This is created automatically when you use the site.
  • Visitor statistics (only with your consent): which pages you view, how you found the site, device and browser type and approximate location at country or city level

We get the data from you or from the shop that uses Resella.

Giving us data is voluntary. Without an email address, however, we cannot add you to the waitlist or reply to you. A customer contract requires the contact person's and invoicing details.

You can join the waitlist yourself if you are at least 13. For anyone younger, a parent or guardian gives consent.

5. Why we process data

  • Demos and enquiries: we reply to you and arrange the demo you asked for (steps before a contract and legitimate interest: staying in touch with businesses interested in our service)
  • Waitlist: we tell you when Resella opens in your area and use postal codes to choose the first areas (consent, which you can withdraw at any time)
  • Newsletter and Kirppiskatsaus report: we send you the emails you have subscribed to (consent, which you can withdraw at any time)
  • Customer relationship: we provide the service to our customers and invoice it (contract)
  • Accounting: we keep accounts and meet other legal obligations (law)
  • Visitor statistics: we see how many people visit the site and which pages interest them, so we can improve the site and our marketing (consent, which you can withdraw at any time)
  • Operation and security: we keep the site and service running and prevent misuse (legitimate interest: a safe and working service)

Our legitimate interest: we want to reply to second-hand shops interested in our service and keep the site safe. We process only a little data for this, in a way you can expect when you contact us. We have assessed that it does not override your interests. You can ask for our assessment at info@resella.fi.

6. Use of AI

We use AI in our work and in our service, and we are open about it.

  • Price suggestion from a photo: when you ask for a price suggestion, the photo of the item and its details, such as category and condition, are sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service. The service returns a price suggestion. The suggestion is only a help, and a person always decides the price. The photo is not used for anything else, so take a photo of the item, not of people.
  • Assistant: the assistant in the system is AI, and this is always shown when you use it. The assistant answers questions based on the user guides. It can be wrong, so check important matters with us. You can always get an answer from a person too.
  • Images and videos: some of the images and videos on our website, social media, emails and marketing are fully or partly made with AI. AI may be used to create an image or video from scratch, to edit a real photo or to add things to it. If an AI-made image or video could look like a real recording of real people, places or events, we also label it where it is shown.
  • Texts: we use AI to help write texts and guides. A person checks everything before it is published.

How the data is processed technically:

  • The AI services are language models from external providers, which we use through an API.
  • The providers do not use the data we send to train AI. They keep it only for a short time to monitor misuse and then delete it.
  • We only send the AI the data the task needs. We do not send sellers' contact details, bank details or sales history.
  • Assistant conversations are kept for up to 90 days so we can fix errors and improve the guides.

We do not use AI to make decisions that would have legal or other significant effects on you. Our use of AI does not fall under the high-risk uses of the AI Act.

7. Who processes the data

We do not sell your data or hand it over for marketing. We use trusted service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Newsletter, waitlist and Kirppiskatsaus report: MailerLite Limited, Ireland. The data is stored in the EU.
  • Demo bookings: Cal.com, Inc., United States. The transfer is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and, in addition, the EU standard contractual clauses.
  • Calendar: Google, United States. The transfer is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Visitor statistics (Google Analytics), only with your consent: Google Ireland Limited, Ireland. Google may also process data in the United States, and the transfer is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising, and Google signals and ad features are turned off.
  • Server and website hosting, payments and banking, AI services and accounting: providers with whom we have a data processing agreement.

If data is transferred outside the EU and EEA, the transfer always uses a safeguard approved by the EU. You can ask for a copy of the safeguards at info@resella.fi.

8. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries and demos: up to 2 years from the last contact
  • Waitlist: until you unsubscribe, or until your area has opened and we have told you
  • Newsletter and Kirppiskatsaus report: until you unsubscribe
  • Assistant conversations: up to 90 days
  • Visitor statistics: 14 months
  • Customer relationship and use of the service: for the duration of the contract and after that as long as the law requires
  • Accounting: invoices and vouchers 6 years, and books and financial statements 10 years from the end of the financial year (Finnish Accounting Act)

9. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising cookies. The site stores this in your browser:

  • Your cookie choice (necessary): remembers whether you accepted statistics or chose only necessary, so we do not ask every time. Kept until you clear your browser data.
  • Google Analytics cookies (only with your consent): _ga and _ga_*, which tell visits apart. Kept for up to 2 years.

Google Analytics is loaded only after you accept statistics. Before you choose, the site does not contact Google. You can change your choice at any time from the Cookie settings link at the bottom of the page. If you withdraw consent, we delete the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

If you came to the site from an ad or QR code link, the campaign information travels from page to page in the address bar. It is not stored in your browser. It is added to your waitlist sign-up only if you send it (legitimate interest: knowing which marketing works).

Fonts and images come from our own server. The only third-party service on the site is Google Analytics, and only if you accept it.

10. Your rights

Your right to object. You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest. You can always refuse direct marketing, and we stop it at once. Email info@resella.fi.

You also have the right to:

  • find out what data we have about you
  • correct wrong data
  • ask us to delete your data
  • restrict processing
  • move your data to another service
  • withdraw your consent, for example leave the waitlist or the newsletter. Every message we send has a link to unsubscribe. Withdrawing does not affect what was done with the data before.

Send your request to info@resella.fi. We reply within one month. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.

11. Security

Only people who need the data for their work can access it. We use encrypted connections and strong authentication.

12. Changes

We update this policy when our service changes. The latest version is always on this page. We will update it before the consumer app launches.
